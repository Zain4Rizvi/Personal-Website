## Problem

A table of tickers hides the shape of a market. Company size, the past year, and how much you pay for each dollar of profit should be readable at a glance, in plain English, without a trading terminal.

## Approach

Maple Market draws large TSX names as a sky. Bigger planets are bigger companies. Green is a stronger past year, red a weaker one, and distance from the sector sun is the price you pay for each dollar of profit. Search and the sector filters find a name. Clicking a planet opens the ticker, the price, and the past-year change.

The same names are also a treemap heatmap. Compare lines up to three companies as a report card. Beside that: a practice portfolio, a replay of prices already on record, a dividend reinvestment sketch, a compound-growth band, and a short quiz.

The public site is labeled demo data. It does not buy or sell anything, and it does not predict where prices go.

## Key technical decision

Put size, past-year colour, and valuation in a React Three Fiber scene instead of columns. The chart pages use Recharts. Zustand holds what is selected.
